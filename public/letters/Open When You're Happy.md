**Open When You're Happy 🤍**

My dearest Yamiki,

If you're reading this, I hope you're smiling right now.

And honestly, just knowing that you're happy makes something inside me feel lighter too.

I don't know what happened today. Maybe you achieved something you've been working for, maybe someone made you laugh, maybe you had a beautiful day, or maybe you're simply sitting somewhere with that cute smile of yours for no particular reason.

Whatever it is, **please don't let this moment pass too quickly.**

Stop for a second.

Take it in.

Look at yourself and realize that you deserve moments like this.

You've been through days that were much heavier than this one. You've cried, you've overthought, you've felt lost, you've questioned yourself, and somehow you still kept going.

So when happiness finds you, Yami...

**don't be scared of it.**

Let yourself be happy.

Laugh loudly.

Be stupid.

Dance for no reason.

Eat something you love.

Tell me everything.

And if I'm not there when you're reading this, imagine me sitting beside you, probably annoying you, probably asking *"What happened? Why are you smiling like an idiot?"* and then secretly smiling because I already know that seeing you happy is enough for me.

You know, one of the things I love most about you isn't just your smile.

It's the person behind it.

The girl who keeps going even when life gets difficult.

The girl who cares too much.

The girl whose heart is sometimes her biggest weakness but also her greatest strength.

The girl who somehow became my home without even realizing it.

So whenever you're happy, I want you to remember this:

**You don't have to earn happiness.**

You don't have to justify it.

You don't have to feel guilty for enjoying a good day just because you've had bad ones before.

You deserve the good days too.

And if someday you look back at this moment years from now, I hope you remember that there was someone who genuinely wanted to see you happy—not only when you were with him, not only when things were perfect, but simply because **you were you.**

I hope we get thousands of these moments together.

Random laughs at 2 AM.

Stupid inside jokes that nobody else understands.

Road trips where we get lost.

Songs that eventually become *our songs.*

Photos that we keep for years.

Quiet mornings.

Big achievements.

Small celebrations.

And those completely ordinary days that somehow become our favourite memories.

And Yami...

If I'm lucky enough to be beside you when you're happy, I'll celebrate with you.

If I'm not, I'll still be happy knowing that you're smiling somewhere.

Because your happiness was never supposed to depend on my presence.

I want to be a part of it, not the reason you need it.

So today, my love, just be happy.

Don't overthink.

Don't worry about tomorrow.

Don't think about the fights.

Don't think about the past.

Just live this one beautiful moment.

And smile a little more.

Because somewhere, there's an idiot who would probably give anything just to see that smile right now.

**I'm proud of you.**

I'm grateful for you.

And more than anything...

I'm happy that you're happy.

Now go enjoy your day, meri Yami.

And please remember—

**your happiness will always have a little corner of my heart reserved for it.** ❤️

Forever yours,

**Aditya**
