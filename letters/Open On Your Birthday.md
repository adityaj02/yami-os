**✉ Open On Your Birthday**

My dearest Yamiki,

Happy birthday, my love. ❤️

If you're reading this, then today is officially your day.

And although I wish I could be standing right in front of you, holding your hands and watching that beautiful smile of yours in person, I hope these words can sit beside you for a little while.

First of all, I want you to know something very simple:

**I'm so grateful that you were born.**

Because somewhere in this ridiculously huge world, on this particular day, a girl was born who would eventually become one of the most important people in my life.

I didn't know you would exist.

I didn't know I'd meet you.

I didn't know I'd fall for you.

I didn't know that one day your smile would become something I'd actively look forward to.

But somehow, life brought you to me.

And I'm grateful for that every single day.

Yami, today I don't want you to think about what you haven't achieved yet.

Don't think about the things that went wrong.

Don't think about the people who hurt you.

Don't think about the future you're scared of.

Today, just celebrate **you**.

The girl who has survived things she rarely talks about.

The girl who has cried and still found reasons to smile.

The girl who cares too deeply.

The girl who sometimes overthinks everything.

The girl with the cutest nakhre.

The girl whose heart is much softer than she lets people believe.

That girl deserves to be celebrated.

And I hope this next year of your life is gentler with you.

I hope you laugh more.

I hope you travel more.

I hope you accomplish the things you've secretly wished for.

I hope you find yourself becoming more confident, more peaceful, and more certain of how incredible you actually are.

And selfishly...

I hope I get to witness a lot of it.

I want to see your little victories.

I want to hear about your random achievements.

I want to celebrate your birthdays with you.

I want to take stupid pictures.

I want to annoy you.

I want to take you on those bike trips we've talked about.

I want us to collect so many memories that one day we'll sit together and say,

*"Remember when we thought that was the biggest problem in our lives?"*

I want the future to be full of those ordinary moments that somehow become our favourite ones.

Maybe one day I'll be sitting beside you on your birthday, holding your hand instead of writing this letter.

Maybe I'll be the annoying person reminding you that you're getting older.

Maybe I'll bring you something you pretend not to like but secretly love.

Maybe we'll argue about where to eat.

Maybe you'll call me stupid.

And I'll probably still look at you and think,

**"How did I get this lucky?"**

But whatever happens, I hope you always remember this birthday for one reason:

You are loved.

Not because you're perfect.

Not because of what you achieve.

Not because of what you can give someone.

Just because you're **Yamiki.**

And that's enough.

So make a wish today.

A really big one.

Don't make it small because you're scared it won't happen.

Ask life for everything you want.

Dream ridiculously.

Because you deserve a life bigger than your fears.

And if someday you forget how special you are, come back to this letter.

Read it again.

And remember that there was once someone who looked at you and thought,

*"This girl is worth celebrating."*

Happy birthday, my Yami.

May this year bring you **sukoon, laughter, love, success, adventures, and a thousand reasons to smile.**

And may I be lucky enough to be there for as many of them as life allows.

I love you.

Happy birthday, meri jaan. 🎂❤️

**Keep smiling. Keep dreaming. Keep being you.**

And please...

save me the biggest piece of cake. 😭

**Forever yours,  
Aditya** ❤️
