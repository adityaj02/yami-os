**✉ Open When You Miss Me**

My dearest Yamiki,

So... you miss me, huh? 🥺❤️

If you're reading this, I wish I could magically appear beside you right now. No dramatic entrance, no cheesy Bollywood background music—bas quietly tumhare paas aa jaun, tumhara haath pakadun, aur tumhe apne paas bitha loon.

I know what it feels like to miss someone.

It's strange, isn't it?

You're doing completely normal things—studying, eating, scrolling through your phone, lying in bed—and suddenly something reminds you of them.

A song.

A place.

A stupid joke.

A smell.

A random thought.

And suddenly, you wish they were there.

So if that's happening right now...

I'm probably missing you too.

I miss the little things about you more than the big things.

Your random talks.

Your expressions.

The way you arrange your hair.

Your laugh.

The way you say my name.

The little arguments.

The "nothing" when I know something is definitely wrong.

Even your nakhre.

Okay, especially your nakhre. 😭

Sometimes I think I miss you more than your memories.

Because memories are just moments that already happened.

But you...

You're still here.

And that means we still have thousands of moments left to make.

Remember our silly dreams?

The bike trips.

The places we want to visit.

The songs we'll eventually call *ours.*

The photographs we'll keep.

The random food we'll try.

The nights we'll spend talking about absolutely nothing.

The mornings where we'll probably fight over who gets the blanket.

The future we've imagined so many times.

Don't let missing me make you sad.

Let it remind you that there's something beautiful waiting for us.

And until we can be together again...

keep a little piece of me with you.

Maybe in a song.

Maybe in a photograph.

Maybe in one of our stupid conversations.

Or just close your eyes for a second and imagine me sitting beside you.

I'd probably look at you and say,

*"Itna miss kar rahi ho mujhe? Pagal."*

And then I'd pull you into the longest hug.

No words.

Just us.

Yami, distance doesn't change what you mean to me.

Some days we might talk less.

Some days life might get busy.

Some days we'll be irritated with each other.

But none of that changes the fact that you're someone my heart looks for.

You're my home.

And sometimes, home is the very place you miss the most when you're away from it.

So until I can actually be there...

take care of yourself for me.

Eat properly.

Sleep properly.

Don't overthink.

And smile a little.

Because somewhere, your idiot is probably looking at his phone and thinking,

**"I wish my Yami was here."**

I love you.

And yes...

**I miss you too. More than I'm ever going to admit without being forced.** 😭❤️

Until I see you again,

**keep a hug saved for me.**

Forever yours,

**Aditya** ❤️
