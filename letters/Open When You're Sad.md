**✉ Open When You're Sad**

My dearest Yamiki,

If you're reading this, I wish more than anything that I could be there beside you right now.

I wish I could just sit next to you without asking too many questions, hold your hand, pull you into a hug, and let you be sad without feeling like you have to explain yourself.

So before anything else, **please don't force yourself to be okay.**

You don't have to smile for everyone.

You don't have to pretend that nothing hurts.

You don't have to be strong every single day.

It's okay to have a bad day.

It's okay to cry.

It's okay to feel lost.

And it's okay if you don't even know why you're sad.

You are human, Yami. And being sad doesn't make you weak.

I know you've carried things that people don't always see. There are parts of your past that hurt you, memories that still come back, and days when your own thoughts become too loud.

I wish I could go back and stand beside you during those days.

Not to change your past—because I can't.

But just to make sure you never felt completely alone in it.

And maybe that's what I want you to remember most today:

**You are not alone anymore.**

Even if I'm not physically beside you, there is someone somewhere who cares about you deeply.

Someone who wants to listen.

Someone who will sit through your silence.

Someone who doesn't need you to be happy all the time to love you.

Me.

You don't have to give me a perfect version of yourself.

Give me the tired version.

The irritated version.

The crying version.

The overthinking version.

The version that doesn't know what to say.

I'll still want you.

I'll still choose you.

I'll still see my Yami.

And please don't ever think that your sadness makes you a burden.

Your feelings are not an inconvenience.

Your tears aren't something you need to apologize for.

If you need space, I'll respect it.

If you need someone, I'll be there.

If you don't know what you need, that's okay too.

We can figure it out slowly.

Remember all those times you thought you wouldn't get through something?

You did.

You survived every single one.

This day will pass too.

Maybe not immediately.

Maybe tomorrow won't magically make everything better.

But slowly...

one breath,

one hour,

one day at a time,

it will become lighter.

And until then, lean on whoever makes you feel safe. Talk to someone you trust. Cry if you need to. Sleep. Eat something. Go outside. Listen to your favourite song.

And if you want me...

you already know where I am.

I don't want to be the person who tells you *"don't be sad."*

I want to be the person who says,

**"It's okay. I'm here. Take your time."**

Because sometimes we don't need someone to fix our sadness.

We just need someone who isn't scared of sitting beside us while we feel it.

So, my love...

Dry your tears whenever you're ready.

Not because you shouldn't cry,

but because I want to see that beautiful smile of yours return when your heart is ready for it.

And until then, remember this:

You are loved.

You are precious.

You are worthy.

You are enough.

And no bad day can change any of that.

**Tum mera ghar ho, Yami.**

And even when home feels a little dark,

I'll always want the light to find its way back to you.

I love you.

Take your time.

I'm here. ❤️

**— Aditya**
