**✉ Open When You Need Motivation**

My dearest Yamiki,

If you're opening this, I already know something.

You're probably sitting there thinking, *"I can't do this anymore."*

So before you believe that thought, listen to me for a minute.

**You can.**

Maybe not everything at once.

Maybe not today.

Maybe you'll have to take it one tiny step at a time.

But you can.

Yami, I know how much you carry inside your head. You think about your studies, your future, your family, your responsibilities, your dreams... sometimes all at once. And then you look at yourself and wonder whether you're doing enough.

You are.

Please don't measure your worth by one bad day, one bad result, one failed attempt, or one moment where you couldn't give your best.

A bad day doesn't make you a failure.

A setback doesn't erase your progress.

And being tired doesn't mean you've lost.

Sometimes the strongest thing you can do is simply wake up tomorrow and try again.

I want you to remember all the things you've already survived.

There were moments you thought would break you.

They didn't.

There were days when you thought you couldn't keep going.

You did.

And look at you now.

Still here.

Still dreaming.

Still fighting.

Still becoming the woman you're supposed to become.

So don't compare your chapter 3 to someone else's chapter 20.

Your journey is yours.

Take your time.

Study.

Work.

Make mistakes.

Learn.

Start again.

And when you feel like you're falling behind, remember that **slow progress is still progress.**

I don't need you to become successful overnight.

I don't need you to have your entire life figured out right now.

I just want you to keep moving.

One page.

One assignment.

One exam.

One decision.

One day at a time.

And please don't ever think that your dreams are too big.

Dream bigger.

Work harder.

And then let the results take their own time.

I want to see you become everything you've imagined yourself becoming.

Not because I need you to prove anything to me.

But because I know how much potential you carry.

And one day, when you're sitting somewhere you've always dreamed of being, I want you to look back at this version of yourself and say,

*"I'm glad I didn't give up."*

And if today you're tired...

Rest.

Don't quit.

There's a difference.

You can pause.

You can cry.

You can take a breath.

You can have one terrible day.

But tomorrow, get up again.

Because your story isn't ending here.

It's barely beginning.

And whenever you feel like nobody believes in you, remember this:

**I do.**

I believe in your heart.

I believe in your mind.

I believe in the woman you're becoming.

And even when you don't believe in yourself, I'll remind you until you do.

So now...

Put the phone down.

Drink some water.

Take a deep breath.

Fix your hair.

Wipe those tears.

And go make yourself proud.

Not me.

Not anyone else.

**You.**

Because the little girl who once dreamed about the life you're working toward deserves to see you fight for it.

And Yami...

one day, we're going to look back at these difficult days while sitting somewhere peaceful, laughing about how worried we were.

**keep going, meri jaan.**
